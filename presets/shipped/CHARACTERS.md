# Shipped characters

Each preset is tuned to one deliberate character so the set does not ship
variations of a single colour. Regenerate with `tools/curate_shipped.py`.

- **glasshouse** — struck glass: 5.2 kHz bell, resonant comb tail, glassy shimmer
- **harbor-02** — close-mic intimacy: chesty low shelf, short room comb, dry
- **ivory-18** — white-noise texture with a hard edge: bright hiss bed, 13-bit crush, saturated presence
- **lumen-43** — cavernous smear: long feedback comb, almost no EQ, tail-dominated
- **orchid-56** — telephone band: both shelves cut hard, 1.6 kHz resonance, boxed
- **orchid-73** — clean glass: minimal colour, gentle shimmer sheen, airy top
- **quartz-56** — velvet: round low-mid, top rolled off, short comb bloom, no edge
- **sable-11** — grit: driven and torn, 11-bit crush, presence peak at 3.2 kHz
- **solstice-30** — shimmering high halo: octave-up presence layer, fast 9.5 Hz shimmer, bright but soft-edged
- **static-bloom** — broken radio: crushed, buzzing, hissy, unstable tremolo
- **understudy** — behind a door: top cut away, thin and distant, dulled
- **velvet-veil** — breathy veil: dark soft noise under a low-passed voice
